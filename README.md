## The entry

`main.py` writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` (246 characters, UTF-8, no trailing newline):

> The tide comes in over the flat sand, slow as dusk, and the water finds every hollow and fills it without hurry. Far out, the light thins to a single line. Nothing asks anything of you here. The sea keeps its own time, and for a while, so do you.

The house judge is a relaxation judge: the text slows the reader down with a concrete, unhurried image — a tide filling every hollow, light thinning to a line, the sea keeping its own time — showing calm instead of instructing it, and leaving an image that keeps growing after the last line.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
